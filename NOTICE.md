### FFmpeg – corresponding source (GPL-3.0 §6)
The bundled `ffmpeg.exe` is the unmodified gyan.dev essentials build 9.0.2 shipped in the `imageio-ffmpeg 0.8.1`
wheel. Its source code is available from <https://ffmpeg.org/download.html> (release 9.0.2) and its build
configuration from <https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/>. For at least three years after each release, a copy of
that source can also be requested from tuanpa1702@gmail.com, free of charge except for the cost of delivery.

### Qt / PySide6 – LGPL-3.0
Qt and PySide6 are used unmodified and dynamically linked. You can rebuild Screen Recorder against your own Qt
build from this repository, or replace the Qt DLLs in an installed copy
(`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Screen Recorder\_internal`).
